## Empirical Findings

The CAFET experiments provide systematic empirical evidence about what did and did not generalize in cross-dataset feature evaluation. Although the system tracks and embeds rich historical candidate evaluation data, we found that purely structural representation context vectors without knowing the target interaction do not generalize reliably out-of-sample. As a result, the investigated adaptive search strategies did not reliably out-perform a simple random search baseline under strict independent evaluation protocols. These negative findings clarify the boundary of unsupervised utility prediction, providing a valuable foundation for future target-aware research directions.
