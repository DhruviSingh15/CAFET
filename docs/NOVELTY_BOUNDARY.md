# Novelty Boundary

**CRITICAL: NOVELTY IS NOT ASSUMED.**

This is a research prototype. It is explicitly acknowledged that:
- The method is NOT claimed to be novel, state-of-the-art, patentable, or superior.
- Any novelty is currently a RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS.
- Experimental and prior-art analysis are required to support any future claims.
- The combination of system components (meta-features, utility predictor, risk ranking, budgeting) is a proposed mechanism being actively tested for usefulness. A negative result is entirely acceptable and scientifically valid.
